What is Stack Ads?

Stack Ads connects your brand to the world’s most trusted developer community, with ads that appear in developers’ daily workflow on Stack Overflow and the Stack Exchange network.

What audience can I reach on Stack Overflow?

Millions of average monthly unique visitors across 183 communities — 82% of devs visit Stack Overflow multiple times per month, and 62% have an influence in the purchase of new technology.

How do I advertise on Stack Overflow?

Get in touch with our team. Your dedicated success manager will help define your goals, build a strategy, and check in regularly to improve your campaign.

What types of advertising formats are available?

Banner advertising, native advertising, Topic Tag sponsorships, site sponsorships, dedicated company pages and sponsored media.

What are the available targeting options?

You can run targeted campaigns based on skills, tags and interests — so you reach more of the right people.
